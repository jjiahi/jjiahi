### Jiangyong Jia

Professor of Physics at Stony Brook University, working on high-energy
heavy-ion collisions at RHIC (STAR) and the LHC (ATLAS).

- **Collective flow**: flow harmonics, longitudinal decorrelations,
  and nonflow subtraction in small and large systems
- **Imaging-by-smashing**: using heavy-ion collisions to extract
  nuclear shapes (deformation, triaxiality, neutron skin)
